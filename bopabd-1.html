We represent the Bangladesh Baha'i Community in its relations with government, the media and civil society.

“In every country or government where any of this community reside, they must behave toward that government with faithfulness, trustfulness, and truthfulness.” – Baha’u’llah

As part of its efforts to contribute to social transformation, the Bahá’ís of Bangladesh participate in public discourses related to the betterment of society. Working shoulder to shoulder with various groups and organizations, the community strives to contribute to the advancement of thought in areas such as the equality of women and men, the role of religion in advancing the common good, media and social cohesion, peace, good governance, well-being of families and development.

The Office of Public Affairs - an agency of the National Spiritual Assembly of the Bahá’ís of Bangladesh, the official governing body of the Bahá’ís of Bangladesh - collaborates with other social actors in finding unifying and constructive approaches to advancing thought in a range of areas vital to social progress.

Empowering Youth as Agents of Positive Social Change

Youth represent a vibrant force for positive transformation and moral leadership within their local communities across Bangladesh. Our Youth Empowerment programs focus on enhancing their capacity to analyze social dynamics, articulate constructive solutions, and organize community service projects.

Through structured peer mentoring, spiritual discourse, and social action, young people refine their intellectual capacities and develop a deep commitment to the welfare of others. Participating youth emerge as active agents of positive change, leading local educational initiatives and fostering unity within their neighborhoods.

By engaging youth in meaningful dialogue and decision-making processes, we nurture a generation capable of serving their communities with integrity, dedication, and selfless devotion.

Promoting Gender Equality and Spiritual Advancement

The full equality of women and men is an essential prerequisite for a just and peaceful society in Bangladesh. Our initiatives foster spaces for open dialogue, education, and social action that highlight the vital role of women in economic, cultural, and spiritual life.

We collaborate with grassroots institutions, educators, and civil society partners to eliminate traditional barriers restricting female leadership. By supporting educational access and economic self-reliance, we work toward a future where women and men contribute as equal partners in all spheres of human endeavor.

Nurturing Moral and Intellectual Capacities in Children

Children possess the potential to become the bright lamps of future society when provided with holistic guidance and moral education. Our educational framework integrates character development with fundamental intellectual skills to help children cultivate virtues such as truthful practice, kindness, and cooperation.

By engaging families and community volunteers, we establish local learning environments where every child feels valued, safe, and inspired. These endeavors nurture moral clarity, mutual respect, and social responsibility from an early age, establishing a firm foundation for harmonious communities.

History

The Baha'i Office of Public Affairs in Bangladesh was established to represent the Baha'i community in its relations with government agencies, civil society organizations, and academic institutions. Over the decades, our office has actively engaged in promoting peaceful coexistence, religious harmony, and gender equality across the nation. Rooted in the principle of the oneness of humanity, we continuously strive to serve as a bridge of cooperation and constructive dialogue in Bangladesh's evolving social landscape.

Approach

Our approach is grounded in the conviction that meaningful social transformation requires both material and spiritual insights working in harmony. We engage in public discourses not through confrontation or political advocacy, but by offering learning-oriented frameworks that encourage unity and shared responsibility. By collaborating with diverse stakeholders across Bangladesh, we seek to identify underlying principles that generate unity of thought and collective action for the common good.
